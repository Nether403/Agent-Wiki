# UX Microcopy & Anti-Cliché Writing Skill

This skill enforces high-signal, concise, and professional product microcopy while eliminating common AI writing clichés (SLOP-022, SLOP-036, SLOP-047).

---

## 1. Banned AI Writing Clichés

Reject generic, hyperbolic placeholder copy generated by LLMs:

| Banned AI Trope | Why It Fails | Zero-Slop Replacement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"In today's fast-paced world..."* | Empty filler; delays communicating value. | State the direct utility immediately. |
| *"Unleash the power of AI..."* | Overused marketing cliché. | *"Run 12 concurrent agent workflows."* |
| *"The future is here..."* | Vague promise with zero specifics. | *"Deterministic zero-slop UI components."* |
| *"99.9% Faster / 100% Guaranteed"* | Hallucinated unprovable metrics (SLOP-036/047). | Real measured benchmark data or configurable props. |

---

## 2. Microcopy Writing Principles

1. **Front-Load Value**: Put the action verb and outcome first: *"Export tokens"* instead of *"Click here to begin exporting your design tokens"*.
2. **Context-Aware Empty States**: Explain **what** is empty, **why** it is empty, and provide **one primary action**:
   - Title: *"No pipelines configured yet"*
   - Body: *"Connect a repository or choose a starter template to trigger your first build."*
   - CTA: `[Create pipeline]`
3. **Actionable Error Messages**: State what happened and how to fix it without technical jargon:
   - Bad: *"Error 500: Internal server malfunction."*
   - Good: *"Unable to load component schemas. Check your internet connection and retry."*
4. **Accessible Button Labels**: Icon-only buttons must have unambiguous aria-labels (`aria-label="Copy component markup to clipboard"`).
